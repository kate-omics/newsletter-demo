What is Vitamin D?

Vitamin D is a fat-soluble vitamin that helps your body absorb calcium and supports healthy bones, muscles, and immune function.

Why is it important?

  • Supports bone health
  • Helps maintain muscle strength
  • Supports immune function
  • Helps regulate calcium levels

Common Causes of Low Vitamin D

  • Limited sunlight exposure
  • Low dietary intake
  • Older age
  • Certain gastrointestinal disorders
  • Kidney or liver disease

Food Sources

  • 🐟 Salmon
  • 🥚 Egg yolks
  • 🥛 Fortified milk
  • 🐠 Sardines
  • 🍄 Mushrooms exposed to UV light

Understanding Your Lab Result

Vitamin D is commonly measured using the 25-hydroxyvitamin D (25(OH)D) blood test. Your healthcare provider will interpret your result based on your laboratory's reference range and your overall health.

Takeaway

Maintaining healthy Vitamin D levels supports bone, muscle, and immune health. If your laboratory result is outside the reference range, discuss it with your healthcare provider before starting supplements or making major lifestyle changes.
